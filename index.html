About

I'm happiest when a project forces me to understand something all the way down. At UCSB I work with the High Energy Physics group on the MilliQan experiment, studying how temperature shifts the signals a detector records from cosmic-ray muons. On the Gaucho Rocket Project I design the avionics that read combustion-chamber temperatures during hot-fire tests.

Outside the lab I write research on how memory changes chatbot performance, build an 8-bit CPU with my team, and mentor students through ACM's research branch. Gautam City is where all of it lives.

Muon Detector & MilliQan Research

Studying how temperature shifts detector readings for cosmic-ray muons in the MilliQan experiment.

Computer Architecture · Team Build

8-Bit CPU from Scratch

A team-built 8-bit computer based on Ben Eater's bus architecture, wired module by module and carried over to an FPGA.

  • Shared 8-bit bus with registers, ALU, RAM and program counter
  • Control logic and microcode for the instruction set
  • Debugged with a logic analyzer and bench supply
Digital LogicFPGAArchitecture

Gaucho Rocket Project · Avionics

Rocket Avionics & Telemetry

Flight electronics for telemetry and data acquisition, including a board that records combustion-chamber temperature during hot-fire tests.

  • Thermocouple DAQ PCB in KiCad (STM32 + MAX31856)
  • Logs chamber temperatures to microSD so data survives the test
  • Onboarding task for new avionics members
